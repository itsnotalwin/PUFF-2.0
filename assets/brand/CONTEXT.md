# Official PUFF logo and social kit

The **final PUFF-2.0 site** is the canonical source for these visual assets. Four full/wordmark master variants are exported as SVG and PNG, with 40 derived platform-size PNGs and three ZIP archives. Source components: `src/lib/bud.ts` and `src/components/puffPaths.ts`. Export scripts: `scripts/extract-puff-art.mjs` and `scripts/export-puff-assets.py`. Regenerate rather than editing generated images directly. Original black/white artwork must remain recognizable and readable at social-avatar sizes.
