# PUFF brand asset specification

Monochrome vector logo from the final PUFF-2.0 website, retaining the original script letter shapes and the existing hand-drawn bud. Versions: white-on-black and black-on-white, each as full emblem and standalone wordmark. SVGs are vector masters and PNGs are opaque white/black exports, not transparent cutouts. Square avatar outputs place the logo well inside the circular crop. Social story, post and banner outputs are centred, never stretched. SVG and PNG assets are generated without changing the site's layout, fonts, animations or order actions.
